## Boundaries

Demon Mastery is ordinary Warlock demon summoning; Enslave Demon is the custom permanent companion package. Neither is stock temporary Enslave Demon (1098). Do not merge them. Feed Demon 894 and Feed Undead 895 are not in the selected modern bundle definitions. Mend Pet is not part of the imported Beast package. Tether Elemental uses tame action 93569, not older 91603.

Wild Imps were not imported as a pet-management bundle. Hand of Gul'dan 954611 describes temporary combat summons; appearance scroll 83141 is a vanity record. They require separate research, not an invented tame/recall lifecycle.
